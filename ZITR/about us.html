ABOUT ZITR

ZITR is a contemporary streetwear brand built around individuality, confidence, and self-expression. We create pieces that blend clean design with bold energy, made for people who aren't afraid to stand out.

Every zitr piece is designed with intention from the fit and details to the overall asethetic. Our goal is simple: create clothing that feels different, looks timeless, and speaks for itself.
